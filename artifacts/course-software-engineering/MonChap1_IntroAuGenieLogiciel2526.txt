   ❑ Vendu sur le marché

       ◼ machine à laver, télévision, lecteur DVD, téléphone mobile,

            ...

Stop Vibe Coding: Reasons to Reconsider

                                                              • Increased complexity in debugging

                                                                                      • Code written impulsively or intuitively can be hard to trace and fix
                                                                                             due to lack of structure.

                                                              • Higher risk of subtle bugs and unpredictable behavior

                                                                                      • Without careful planning, vibe coding can lead to fragile
                                                                                             implementations.

                                                              • Unclear learning path for new developers

                                                                                      • The absence of consistent patterns or documentation makes
                                                                                             onboarding and collaboration difficult.

                                                              • Difficulty in tracing and reproducing errors

                                                                                      • Improvised code often lacks proper logging or version control,
                                                                                             making issues harder to isolate.
